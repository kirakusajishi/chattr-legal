Delete Your Account

⚠️ Deleting your account is permanent. This action cannot be undone.

What will be deleted

  • Your account (Firebase Authentication)
  • Your profile information
  • Your messages (Firestore)
  • Your uploaded files (Firebase Storage)
  • Your call history and metadata

What may be retained

  • Data required for legal compliance
  • Anonymous technical logs (if applicable)

How to delete your account

You can request account deletion by contacting us:

Email: chattr.appx@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Your account email
  • Username (if applicable)
  • Request: "Delete my Chattr account"

Processing time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 7–30 days.
